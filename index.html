Urinary Stone Mini Notes

High-yield concepts for your Genitourinary Block.

01

Calcium Oxalate

The most common type of urinary stone. Associated with hypercalciuria, hyperoxaluria, and hypocitraturia.

Microscopic appearance

Envelope-shaped or dumbbell-shaped crystals.

02

Uric Acid

Associated with acidic urine, hyperuricosuria, and increased uric acid concentration.

Microscopic appearance

Rhomboid or rosette-shaped crystals.

03

Struvite

Composed of magnesium ammonium phosphate. Associated with urease-producing bacteria.

Microscopic appearance

Coffin-lid shaped crystals.

04

Cystine

Associated with cystinuria, an inherited defect in renal tubular amino acid transport.

Microscopic appearance

Hexagonal-shaped crystals.

CLINICAL PEARLS

Remember These!

  • Colicky flank pain radiating to the groin is typical of ureteric stones.
  • Hematuria may occur in patients with urinary stones.
  • Struvite stones are associated with urease-producing bacteria.
  • Urine pH is an important factor in stone formation.
  • Non-contrast CT is a highly sensitive imaging modality for urinary stones in adults.
